Account and data deletion

Last updated 6 November 2026

Current limitation: Self-service account deletion is not implemented. You can contact the operator by email to request assistance. This page does not automatically delete an account or submit a request for you.

How to request deletion

Email vikas.sharma7010@gmail.com from your registered account email where possible. Use the subject “Sanyog account deletion request” and identify the account using the minimum information needed. Never send your password or OTP.

What deletion should cover

A complete process should identify and remove or de-identify the account record, profile details, uploaded photographs, authentication account and related personal data, subject to lawful retention requirements. Backups, security logs and records that must be retained may follow a documented retention schedule.

Verification and timing

The operator should verify that the request comes from the account holder, explain any information that must be retained and provide a completion confirmation. A response timeline and escalation route must be confirmed and communicated.

Before requesting deletion

Save any information you need. Deletion may be irreversible. Do not assume uninstalling the PWA or signing out deletes your account or stored data.

Data export

Self-service data export is not currently available. For access or copy requests, email vikas.sharma7010@gmail.com.
