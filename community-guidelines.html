Community Guidelines

Draft for review · Last updated 6 November 2026

Sanyog is intended to support respectful, consensual connections between adults. These rules apply to profiles, photographs, communications and any future member features.

Be truthful

Use your own identity and accurate details. Do not impersonate someone, misrepresent age or marital status, fabricate credentials or create a profile for another person without informed consent.

Respect consent and autonomy

Do not pressure anyone to respond, share photographs, meet, marry or disclose personal information. A family member's involvement must be authorised by the candidate. Consent can be withdrawn.

No harassment or hate

Do not threaten, stalk, intimidate, blackmail, shame or repeatedly contact someone who has declined contact. Do not publish abusive or demeaning content targeting protected or personal characteristics.

No fraud or exploitation

Do not request money, gifts, loans, financial credentials, OTPs or intimate images under false pretences. Do not promote investment schemes, fake emergencies, trafficking, coercion or any exploitative activity.

Protect privacy

Do not share another person's phone number, address, photographs, documents, screenshots or private messages without permission. Never publish identity documents or sensitive personal data in a profile.

Use photographs lawfully

Upload only photographs you have the right and permission to use. Do not upload another person's photos as your own, manipulated intimate imagery, or images of minors.

Enforcement and reporting

Violations may lead to content removal or account restrictions when moderation tools and procedures are operational. Dedicated reporting, blocking, appeal and response workflows are still being built. Until then, contact [SAFETY CONTACT TO BE ADDED] when published. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
