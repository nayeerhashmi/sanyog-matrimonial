4. Sensitive and optional details

Religion, caste, community, tribe, language, marital status and similar details are optional where offered. Sanyog should not infer these details from a person's name, location or appearance. Only provide information you are comfortable sharing.

5. Photos and visibility

The member photo bucket is configured as private. Access is intended to be restricted by account permissions. Do not assume a photograph is publicly visible or share private photo links. Future display of profile photos will require a clear feature, user choice and appropriate access controls.

These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms. The operator may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. No sale of personal information is intended.
