Before you connect

  • Keep your account password and OTP private.
  • Do not share financial information, identity documents or your home address in public profile fields.
  • Be cautious if someone quickly asks for money, secrecy, urgent help or a move to an untraceable channel.
  • Verify important claims independently. A profile is not proof of identity, character or marital status.

When speaking with someone

Use your own judgement and move at a pace that feels comfortable. Do not tolerate pressure, threats, blackmail or demands for intimate images. Keep control of your own account and communications.

Meeting in person

  • Choose a public place and arrange your own transport.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going and when you expect to return.
  • Keep your phone charged and do not feel obliged to continue a meeting.
  • Do not send money or hand over documents because someone asks in person.

If you suspect fraud or abuse

Stop contact, preserve relevant messages and payment records, and avoid confronting someone if it could put you at risk. Contact local emergency services if there is immediate danger. In India, cyber financial fraud can be reported through the official national cybercrime portal or helpline 1930; verify current official channels before submitting details.
